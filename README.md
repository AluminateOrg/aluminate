# AluminateOrg
Aluminate is an innovative alumni management platform designed to strengthen the connection between educational institutions and their graduates. It provides a seamless and engaging digital environment where alumni, students, and administrators can interact, collaborate, and contribute to community growth.

With Aluminate, institutions can manage alumni networks, organize professional groups, and analyze engagement through powerful dashboards. The platform also simplifies donation management by allowing users to browse active campaigns, track progress, and contribute securely through integrated payment gateways.
